Lakeland Financial Reports Record Third Quarter Performance with Loan Growth of 8%; Diluted Earnings per Share Increases by 11%
Warsaw, Indiana (October 26, 2026) – Lakeland Financial Corporation (Nasdaq Global Select/LKFN), parent company of Lake City Bank, today reported record third quarter net income of $28.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, which represents an increase of $2.5 million, or 9%, compared to net income of $26.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Diluted earnings per share of $1.14 for the third quarter of 2026 also represents a record for third quarter performance and increased $0.11, or 11%, compared to $1.03 for the third quarter of 2025. On a linked quarter basis, net income increased $417,000 from $28.4 million. Diluted earnings per share increased $0.01 from $1.13 on a linked quarter basis.
The company further reported record performance year-to-date with net income of $83.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, versus $73.5 million for the corresponding period of 2025, an increase of $10.3 million, or 14%. Diluted earnings per share increased $0.46, or 16%, to $3.31 for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, versus $2.85 for the corresponding period of 2025.
Total revenue was $73.0 million for the third quarter of 2026 representing an increase of $4.0 million, or 6%, as compared to $69.0 million for the third quarter of 2025. On a linked quarter basis, total revenue increased by $2.1 million, or 3%, from $70.9 million in the second quarter of 2026. Total revenue increased by $14.4 million, or 7%, to $213.6 million for nine months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $199.2 million for the corresponding period of 2025.
"Strong loan growth and healthy net interest margin expansion were reflected in the quality of our earnings performance in the quarter," commented David M. Findlay, Chairman and CEO. "Clearly, the resulting record net income reflects the efforts of the entire Lake City Bank team as we continue to grow our market share in every market we serve."

Net Interest Margin
Net interest margin was 3.58% for the third quarter of 2026, representing an 8 basis point increase from 3.50% for the third quarter of 2025. Net interest margin was favorably impacted by a reduction in deposit pricing that outpaced the decline in earning asset yields. The company's funding costs fell by 24 basis points from 2.37% for the third quarter of 2025 to 2.13% for the third quarter of 2026. Offsetting the decrease in funding costs was a decrease to earning asset yields of 16 basis points from 5.87% for the third quarter of 2025 to 5.71% for the third quarter of 2026.
Net interest margin increased 9 basis points to 3.58% for the third quarter of 2026, as compared to 3.49% for the linked second quarter of 2026. Average earning asset yields increased by 6 basis points from 5.65% to 5.71% on a linked quarter basis and interest expense as a percentage of average earning assets decreased 3 basis points from 2.16% to 2.13%. The second quarter cost of funds were elevated by inflows of seasonal public funds deposits into higher priced deposit products.
Net interest income was $60.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of $4.6 million, or 8%, as compared to $56.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. On a linked quarter basis, net interest income increased by $2.4 million, or 4%, from $58.3 million. Net interest income increased by $12.0 million, or 7%, from $163.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, to $175.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
"We're particularly proud of the net interest margin expansion on a year-over-year basis as well as on a quarter-linked basis. This significant improvement reflects the positive impact of healthy loan growth combined with deposit pricing strategies to positively impact our net interest margin," stated Lisa M. O'Neill, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer.
Capital Strength
The company’s total capital as a percentage of risk-weighted assets was 15.56% at September 30, 2026, compared to 16.21% at September 30, 2025, and 15.61% at June 30, 2026. These capital levels significantly exceeded the 10.00% regulatory threshold required to be characterized as "well capitalized" and reflect the company's robust capital base.
The company’s tangible common equity to tangible assets ratio, which is a non-GAAP financial measure, was 10.11% at September 30, 2026, compared to 10.79% at September 30, 2025, and 10.63% at June 30, 2026. The decline in the tangible common equity to tangible assets ratio was primarily driven by an increase in unrealized losses from available-for-sale investment securities, resulting from the rapid 85 basis point rise in the 10-year Treasury yield during the third quarter. As a result, unrealized losses on available-for-sale investment securities increased to $213.9 million at September 30, 2026, up from $159.9 million at September 30, 2025, and $140.9 million at June 30, 2026. Excluding the impact of accumulated other comprehensive income (loss) on tangible common equity and tangible assets, the company’s ratio of adjusted tangible common equity to adjusted tangible assets, a non-GAAP financial measure, was 12.32% at September 30, 2026, compared to 12.57% at September 30, 2025, and 12.14% at June 30, 2026.
The company continued to repurchase shares during the third quarter, with 41,550 shares of its common stock repurchased at a weighted average price per share of $58.45 with an aggregate purchase price of $2.4 million. The current program authorizes the company to repurchase up to $60.0 million in aggregate purchase price of the company's common stock through April 30, 2027, or 8.2% of current equity. The company has repurchased a total of 787,166 shares under the current program from April 1, 2025 through September 30, 2026 at an aggregate purchase price of $45.4 million and weighted average purchase price per share of $57.62, with remaining authorization of $14.6 million at quarter end.
As announced on October 13, 2026, the board of directors approved a cash dividend for the third quarter of $0.52 per share, payable on November 5, 2026, to shareholders of record as of October 25, 2026. The third quarter dividend per share represents a 4% increase from the $0.50 dividend per share paid for the third quarter of 2025. The dividend payout ratio improved to 47.1% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to 52.6% for the similar period in 2025.
Kristin L. Pruitt, President, noted, "We are pleased to continue to opportunistically leverage our share repurchase program to demonstrate our confidence in the quality of our performance and the long-term strength of our model, as well as our earnings potential. Our capital position remains robust, enabling us to grow the balance sheet while also returning excess capital to shareholders through a meaningful dividend as well as the common share repurchases."

Loan Portfolio
Average total loans of $5.59 billion in the third quarter of 2026 increased $381.9 million, or 7%, from $5.21 billion for the third quarter of 2025, and increased $56.4 million, or 1%, from $5.53 billion for the second quarter of 2026.
Total loans, net of deferred loan fees, increased by $431.4 million, or 8%, from $5.25 billion as of September 30, 2025, to $5.68 billion as of September 30, 2026. The growth in loans was driven by increases across all primary commercial and consumer segments of the portfolio, with increases to the commercial and industrial loans of $187.4 million, or 12%, commercial real estate and multi-family residential loans of $117.6 million, or 4%, consumer 1-4 family mortgage loans of $83.2 million, or 16%, other commercial loans of $28.8 million, or 31%, agri-business and agricultural loans of $8.2 million, or 2%, and other consumer loans of $6.7 million, or 6%.
On a linked quarter basis, total loans increased by $100.4 million, or 2%, from $5.58 billion at June 30, 2026. The linked quarter increase was driven by growth in both the commercial and consumer segments of the portfolio, with increases to the commercial and industrial loan portfolio of $61.1 million, or 4%, consumer 1-4 family mortgage loans of $18.0 million, or 3%, commercial real estate and multi-family residential loans of $12.2 million, or less than 1%, and agri-business and agricultural loans of $8.8 million, or 3%.
Commercial loan originations for the third quarter were approximately $525.0 million and were offset by approximately $443.0 million in loan pay downs. Commercial and industrial line of credit usage was 42% as of September 30, 2026, compared to 40% at September 30, 2025, and 43% at June 30, 2026. Total commercial and industrial lines of credit availability grew by $104.0 million, or 5%, as compared to the prior year, and line usage increased by $88.0 million, or 10%, over that period. On a linked quarter basis, total commercial and industrial lines of credit availability declined by $2.0 million, and line usage declined by $16.0 million, or 2%.
"The strength of loan growth during 2026 has been highlighted by double digit growth in commercial and industrial loans. This encouraging trend is further supported by commercial loan originations outpacing loan paydowns for the fourth quarter in a row," stated Findlay. "We continue to make progress on our organic loan growth plans with high single digit loan growth on a year-over-year and quarter-linked bases. We are optimistic that our continued emphasis on client prospecting and joint calling is beginning to yield positive results. We continue to add to our commercial and retail lending teams throughout the Lake City Bank footprint."

Total deposits increased $270.5 million, or 4%, from $6.02 billion as of September 30, 2025, to $6.29 billion as of September 30, 2026. The increase in total deposits was driven by an increase in core deposits, which increased by $204.3 million, or 3%. Conversely, commercial deposits contracted by $78.3 million, or 3%, to $2.21 billion. Brokered deposits increased by $66.2 million, or 38%, but remain at a low level, representing 4% of total deposits at September 30, 2026.
On a linked quarter basis, total deposits decreased $34.8 million, or 1%, from $6.33 billion at June 30, 2026, to $6.29 billion at September 30, 2026. Core deposits increased by $22.5 million, or less than 1%, while brokered deposits decreased by $57.3 million, or 19%. The linked quarter growth in core deposits was driven by an increase in commercial deposits of $96.6 million, or 5%, and an increase in retail deposits of $48.9 million, or 3%. This growth was offset by a seasonal reduction in public funds of $123.0 million, or 6%.
Average total deposits were $6.34 billion for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $308.5 million, or 5%, from $6.03 billion for the third quarter of 2025. Average interest-bearing deposits drove the increase in average total deposits and increased by $259.3 million, or 5%. Contributing to the overall growth of interest-bearing deposits was an increase in total average time deposits of $174.4 million, or 23%, and an increase in average interest-bearing checking accounts of $88.6 million, or 2%. Additionally, average noninterest-bearing demand deposits increased by $49.3 million, or 4%, to $1.29 billion.
On a linked quarter basis, average total deposits increased by $25.4 million, or less than 1%, from $6.31 billion for the second quarter of 2026 to $6.34 billion for the third quarter of 2026. Driving the increase was an increase in noninterest bearing demand deposits of $65.9 million, or 5%, and an increase in average time deposits of $13.3 million, or 1%. Offsetting these increases were reductions in interest bearing checking accounts, which declined by $47.1 million, or 1%, and average savings accounts, which declined by $6.7 million, or 2%.
Checking account growth as of September 30, 2026, compared to September 30, 2025, includes growth of $261.3 million, or 15%, in aggregate public fund checking account balances and growth of $43.5 million, or 5%, in aggregate retail checking account balances. Offsetting these increases was a decline of $128.2 million, or 6%, in aggregate commercial checking account balances. The number of accounts expanded for all three segments, with growth of 2% for commercial accounts, 1% for public funds accounts and 1% for retail accounts.
"Core deposits remain stable at 96% of total deposits," added O'Neill. "During the quarter we deployed deposit growth strategies in Indianapolis that yielded $106.1 million in deposits, of which $83.2 million was new money to Lake City Bank. This strategy contributed to the quarter linked growth in commercial and retail deposits and provides our revenue producing teams with new opportunities to continue to grow deposit and loan relationships."

Asset Quality
The company recorded a provision for credit losses of $1.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $2.0 million in the third quarter of 2025 and $1.7 million for the linked second quarter of 2026.
The allowance for credit loss reserve to total loans was 1.21% at September 30, 2026, down from 1.30% at September 30, 2025, and 1.27% at June 30, 2026. The company recorded net charge offs of $3.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to net charge offs of $384,000 in the third quarter of 2025 and $24,000 during the linked second quarter of 2026. Net charge offs during the third quarter of 2026 were driven by a partial charge off of $2.8 million to address a shortfall in collateral related to the ongoing workout of a nonperforming commercial credit, which was fully reserved for in prior quarters. Annualized net charge offs to average loans were 0.21% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 0.03% for the third quarter of 2025 and 0.00% for the linked second quarter of 2026.
Nonperforming assets decreased by $1.1 million, or 6%, to $18.0 million as of September 30, 2026, versus $19.1 million as of September 30, 2025. On a linked quarter basis, nonperforming assets decreased by $2.0 million, or 10%, primarily driven by the aforementioned $2.8 million partial charge off. The ratio of nonperforming assets to total assets at September 30, 2026, decreased to 0.25%, down from 0.28% at September 30, 2025 and June 30, 2026.
Total individually analyzed and watch list loans increased by $44.7 million, or 28%, to $202.0 million as of September 30, 2026, versus $157.2 million as of September 30, 2025. On a linked quarter basis, total individually analyzed and watch list loans increased by $4.0 million, or 2%, from $198.0 million at June 30, 2026. The increase in individually analyzed and watch list loans during the linked quarter was driven primarily by credit downgrades to the watch list of approximately $15.6 million and offset by payoffs and upgrades of approximately $9.4 million and the $2.8 million partial charge off. Watch list loans as a percentage of total loans were 3.56% at September 30, 2026, a 56 basis point increase compared to 3.00% at September 30, 2025, and a 1 basis point increase from 3.55% at June 30, 2026.
Findlay stated, "The Indiana economy is resilient, diverse and expanding. Our borrowers continue to navigate through the challenges created by rising oil prices and tariff policy uncertainty. We are pleased that our asset quality trends continue to be stable with historically-low troubled loan totals, and we remain optimistic about the manufacturing outlook in our state."
Investment Portfolio Overview

Investment securities represented 15% of total assets as of September 30, 2026, down from 17% at September 30, 2025 and 16% at June 30, 2026. Tax equivalent adjusted effective duration for the investment portfolio contracted to 5.6 years at September 30, 2026, compared to 5.8 years at September 30, 2025, and 5.8 years at June 30, 2026.

Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Tangible common equity, adjusted tangible common equity, tangible assets, adjusted tangible assets, tangible book value per common share, tangible common equity to tangible assets, adjusted tangible common equity to adjusted tangible assets, and pretax pre-provision earnings are non-GAAP financial measures calculated based on GAAP amounts. Tangible common equity is calculated by excluding the balance of goodwill and other intangible assets from the calculation of equity, net of deferred tax. Tangible assets are calculated by excluding the balance of goodwill and other intangible assets from the calculation of total assets, net of deferred tax. Adjusted tangible assets and adjusted tangible common equity remove the fair market value adjustment impact of the available-for-sale investment securities portfolio in accumulated other comprehensive income (loss) ("AOCI"). Tangible book value per common share is calculated by dividing tangible common equity by the number of shares outstanding less true treasury stock. Pretax pre-provision earnings is calculated by adding net interest income to noninterest income and subtracting noninterest expense. Because not all companies use the same calculation of tangible common equity and tangible assets, this presentation may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures calculated by other companies. However, management considers these measures of the company’s value meaningful to understanding of the company’s financial information and performance.
