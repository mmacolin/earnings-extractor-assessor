FitLife Brands Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
OMAHA, NE – November 16, 2026 – FitLife Brands, Inc. (“FitLife” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: FTLF), a provider of innovative and proprietary nutritional supplements and wellness products, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Highlights for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 include:
● | Total revenue was $25.6 million, an increase of 9% compared to the third quarter of 2025, driven by the acquisition of Irwin Naturals, partially offset by lower revenue from Legacy FitLife.
● | Wholesale revenue was $13.8 million, representing 54% of total revenue and an increase of 5% compared to the third quarter of 2025.
● | Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $1.6 million compared to $0.9 million during the same period last year, an increase of 71%, driven by the non-recurrence of merger and acquisition related expense associated with the acquisition of Irwin Naturals during the third quarter of 2025 as well as a lower effective tax rate during the third quarter of 2026, partially offset by a restructuring charge during the third quarter of 2026.
● | Basic earnings per share and diluted earnings per share were $0.17 and $0.16, respectively, compared to $0.10 and $0.09 for the third quarter of 2025.
● | Adjusted EBITDA was $3.3 million, a 13% decrease compared to the third quarter of 2025.
● | The Company ended the quarter with $39.3 million of debt outstanding, and cash of $0.7 million, or total net debt of $38.6 million.
For the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, total revenue increased 9% to $25.6 million compared to $23.5 million during the same period last year, primarily due to the acquisition of Irwin Naturals (“Irwin”), partially offset by lower revenue from Legacy FitLife. Irwin was acquired on August 8, 2025, so the third quarter of 2025 includes the impact of the Irwin acquisition only for the period from August 8 through September 30, 2025.
Wholesale revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $13.8 million, a 5% increase from the same period last year. The Company’s acquisition of Irwin contributed $9.4 million of wholesale revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, while Legacy FitLife wholesale revenue declined $2.3 million, or 34%, compared to the same period last year. The decline in Legacy FitLife wholesale revenue is attributable to lower revenue from certain retail partners, primarily GNC.
1
Online revenue for the quarter was $11.8 million, a 15% increase compared to the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Online revenue accounted for 46% and 44% of the Company’s total revenue during the quarters ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively. Legacy FitLife online revenue declined 18%, primarily attributable to MRC, while Irwin contributed $3.6 million of online revenue.
Gross margin for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was 37.7% compared to 37.2% during the same period in the prior year. Gross margin in the prior-year period was adversely affected by $0.4 million of amortization of inventory step-up. Excluding the step-up amortization, gross margin was 38.9% in the third quarter of 2025.
Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $1.6 million compared to $0.9 million during the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Basic earnings per share and diluted earnings per share were $0.17 and $0.16, respectively, compared to $0.10 and $0.09 for the third quarter of 2025. Net income for the third quarter of 2025 was adversely impacted by merger and acquisition related costs as well as by amortization of the inventory step-up.
Adjusted EBITDA for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $3.3 million, a decrease of 13% compared to $3.8 million in the same period in 2025. Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 excludes $0.3 million of restructuring costs.
The Company ended the third quarter of 2026 with $34.5 million outstanding on its term loan and $4.8 million outstanding on its revolving line of credit. Cash was $0.7 million, resulting in total net debt of $38.6 million, compared to $37.0 million at June 30, 2026.
Since completing the acquisition of Irwin on August 8, 2025, through the end of the third quarter of 2026, the Company has paid off approximately $7.3 million of indebtedness in addition to paying approximately $2.0 million of transaction-related expenses.
During the third quarter of 2026, the Company implemented and substantially completed a restructuring initiative designed to improve operating efficiency and reduce its cost structure. The Company incurred restructuring expense of approximately $0.3 million, primarily severance costs, and expects the initiative to reduce annual operating expense by approximately $1.2 million, with savings beginning in the fourth quarter of 2026.

Performance of Acquired Brands
One of the primary metrics used by management to evaluate the performance of the Company’s brands is contribution, a non-GAAP financial measure which management defines as gross profit less advertising and marketing expenditures. Other companies may also report contribution as a performance metric, but their definition or calculation of contribution may differ from the Company’s. Management believes that contribution, as defined by the Company, is a particularly relevant performance metric since it incorporates the gross profit associated with a specific brand or collection of brands as well as the advertising and marketing expenditures associated with the same brand or brands. With limited exceptions, other operating expense incurred by the Company is generally not allocable to a specific brand or collection of brands.
Management intends to provide this level of disclosure for acquired brands for approximately two years following a transaction, after which the performance of acquired brands will be reported as part of Legacy FitLife results. Legacy FitLife consists of thirteen brands, including MRC and MusclePharm, and Irwin consists of three brands. These collections of brands do not meet the definition of operating segments and are not managed as such.

Management Commentary
Dayton Judd, Chairman and CEO of the Company commented, “Our business continues to be challenged by a weak consumer environment. In addition, we continue to experience challenges unique to some of the categories and channels in which we operate. That said, we have now owned Irwin Naturals for four complete quarters, and we are encouraged that revenue for the Company overall is roughly flat despite the headwinds. In each of the previous four quarters, the Company generated revenue between $25.3 million and $26.5 million and Adjusted EBITDA between $3.3 million and $3.7 million. For the trailing 12 months, revenue is $103.4 million and Adjusted EBITDA is $13.7 million.
“It is important to note that not all brands are declining. Although a weak consumer environment affects all brands, the only areas where we are seeing notable declines are (1) with MRC on Amazon, and (2) in the specialty retail channel, primarily due to GNC for some of our Legacy FitLife brands and the health food channel for Irwin.
“Aside from these challenges, we are encouraged by the performance of the rest of the business. Online revenue continues to grow, with the Irwin and MusclePharm brands delivering particularly strong performance and reaching all-time highs during the third quarter of 2026. MusclePharm continues to improve in the wholesale channel as well, achieving its second highest quarter of wholesale revenue during the third quarter of 2026 at substantially improved gross margins.
5
“In an effort to operate more efficiently in the current environment, the Company undertook a restructuring initiative during the third quarter. The initiative, which was substantially completed by the end of the third quarter, is anticipated to decrease the Company’s annual operating expense by approximately $1.2 million annually beginning in the fourth quarter of 2026. The Company has also completed other initiatives that are anticipated to result in reduced cost of goods sold.
“With regard to the balance sheet, our debt increased slightly during the quarter, driven entirely by temporary fluctuations in working capital. More specifically, compared to the end of the second quarter of 2026, accounts receivable increased by $1.1 million, inventory increased by $1.7 million, and prepaids increased by $0.7 million, while accounts payable increased only $0.3 million. We expect working capital to normalize in the future and debt reduction to continue.
“A substantial portion of the increase in working capital relates to opportunistic, forward purchases or commitment payments for whey protein and fish oil, two commodities that are experiencing dramatic cost increases driven by supply shortages. With payments made during the third quarter (and early in the fourth quarter), the Company has locked in fixed-price supply of fish oil to meet the majority of its needs over approximately the next 12 months.
“To summarize, we continue to experience headwinds in certain parts of our business, but fortunately not in all. And we believe the cost reduction actions we have recently taken will allow the Company to compete more efficiently going forward.”
