SenesTech Reports Record Q3 2026 Revenue with 59% Year-Over-Year Growth

Revenue increased 42% sequentially as e-commerce and subscriptions expanded;

Gross profit reached a record $796,000

SURPRISE, Ariz., November 10, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — SenesTech, Inc. (NASDAQ: SNES), an innovator in rodent control, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Q3 2026 Financial Highlights

•Total revenue increased 42% sequentially to a record $1.097 million in Q3 2026, compared to $770,000 in Q2 2026, and increased 59% compared to $690,000 in Q3 2025. This was the second consecutive quarterly revenue record and the first quarter above $1 million, consistent with the preliminary revenue of approximately $1.1 million announced on October 15, 2026.
•E-commerce revenue increased 52% sequentially and 337% year over year, representing approximately 71% of total Q3 revenue.
•Subscription revenue increased 53% sequentially and 234% year over year. Combined subscribers across Amazon and the Company’s website increased 72% compared to Q2 2026, strengthening the recurring revenue base.
•Direct B2B revenue increased 19% sequentially and 2% year over year. Sequential growth was driven by process changes to the Company’s B2B efforts, which drove growth in the pest management, government and commercial verticals.
•Evolve® revenue increased 67% year over year to $979,000 and represented 89% of product revenue. ContraPest® revenue increased 13% year over year to $118,000, driven by higher refill sales.
•Gross profit increased 40% to a record $796,000 in Q3 2026, compared to $567,000 in Q2 2026, and increased 84% compared to $433,000 in Q3 2025.
•Gross margin was 72.6% in Q3 2026, compared to 73.6% in Q2 2026 and 62.8% in Q3 2025.
•Net loss for Q3 2026 was $2.0 million, compared to $1.8 million in Q2 2026, and $1.3 million in Q3 2025.
•Adjusted EBITDA loss was $1.4 million in Q3 2026, compared to $1.4 million in Q2 2026, and $1.1 million in Q3 2025.
•Cash and cash equivalents decreased $1.1 million during Q3 2026 to $4.0 million.
•Net cash used in operating activities declined each quarter in 2026, falling to $1.3 million in Q3 from $1.7 million in Q2 2026 and $1.9 million in Q1 2026.

Recent Operational and Strategic Highlights

•Amazon Revenue and Subscriptions Continue to Grow: Amazon revenue increased each month of Q3, bringing in new customers and expanding the recurring subscription base.
•B2B Sales Team Expanded: The Company added two salespeople, bringing the team under Executive Vice President of Sales Jack Karabees to four, with two additional hires expected by year end. The team is focused on building repeat business across targeted domestic verticals.


•E-commerce Reach Expanded Through Grove Collaborative: SenesTech added Grove Collaborative as a third-party e-commerce channel, bringing Evolve® to an engaged base of consumers focused on sustainability.
•Assessment and Monitoring Services Launched for B2B Customers: In July, SenesTech began offering assessment services to help B2B customers measure rodent activity and understand the scope of an infestation. Customers can choose an assessment performed by SenesTech or deploy the Company’s tracking products themselves, with results analyzed using SenesTech’s internally developed AI tool. These services complement existing implementation and deployment services and are expected to provide an additional revenue opportunity.
•Municipal Results Support Broader Adoption: Chicago’s Wicker Park Bucktown program, which incorporated SenesTech’s assessment services and Evolve® into an integrated pest management approach, reduced average rat activity intensity by 88% over 12 months. Evolve has expanded to six Chicago-area Special Service Areas and wards. In Swampscott, Massachusetts, Evolve Rat, combined with sanitation and waste management improvements, helped reduce measured rodent activity by 43% over six months.
•Sandstrom Partners Engaged to Support Branding and Marketing: SenesTech engaged Sandstrom Partners, a Portland, Oregon, brand development firm whose clients have included Nike, Target and Coca-Cola, as a strategic advisor on marketing and branding. An early priority is helping retain Amazon customers and attract new customers by more clearly communicating how the Company’s rodent birth control products work.

Management Commentary

“SenesTech delivered a milestone third quarter, exceeding $1 million in quarterly revenue for the first time and setting new records for revenue, gross profit and subscription customers,” said Michael Edell, President and Chief Executive Officer of SenesTech. “These results demonstrate the momentum we are building. With e-commerce delivering strong growth and our expanded B2B sales team pursuing new opportunities, we are excited about the foundation we are establishing for continued growth.

“Our e-commerce business continues to lead the way, with revenue increasing 52% sequentially, driven by another record quarter on Amazon. Across Amazon and our website, combined subscription customers increased 72% sequentially, while subscription revenue grew 53%. We are reaching more customers and building ongoing relationships that support repeat purchases, greater revenue visibility and long-term customer value.

“We see direct B2B as the next significant driver of growth alongside our expanding e-commerce business. By expanding the sales team, we have increased our capacity to pursue opportunities across our targeted verticals. Direct B2B revenue increased 19% sequentially, and we believe our growing brand awareness, broader customer adoption and focused sales efforts position us to build on that progress,” Mr. Edell concluded.

“Record gross profit of $796,000 and a gross margin of 72.6% highlight the strength of our business model as revenue scales,” said Tom Chesterman, Chief Financial Officer of SenesTech. “Our focus is on translating this commercial momentum into sustained financial progress. We are also putting our cash to work more efficiently: excluding non-cash items such as stock-based and other non-cash compensation, depreciation and straight-line rent, sales and marketing has grown as a share of operating expenses compared to the third quarter of 2025, while general and administrative spending has declined as a share.”

About SenesTech

SenesTech is the leader in rodent birth control and sustainable rodent population management, with a mission to support a healthier environment through the responsible management of animal pest populations. The Company’s Evolve® and ContraPest® fertility control solutions, along with its assessment and monitoring services, target reproduction to help reduce rodent populations. SenesTech serves consumer and professional markets with proven approaches that can be used independently or as part of an integrated pest management (IPM) program.

For more information, visit https://senestech.com/.
