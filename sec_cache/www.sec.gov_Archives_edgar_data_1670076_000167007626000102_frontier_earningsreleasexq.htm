Highlights:
•Delivered record revenue of approximately $1.4 billion, up 53 percent versus the corresponding 2025 quarter, driven by strong demand and continued execution of the Company’s commercial strategy
•Increased RASM 30 percent to 11.87 cents, significantly above guidance, on 18 percent higher capacity
•Maintained cost discipline, with total adjusted (non-GAAP) operating expenses of approximately $1.3 billion, or 11.56 cents per ASM, including fuel expense at an average cost of $3.96 per gallon, and adjusted (non-GAAP) CASM - excluding fuel of 7.40 cents, stage-adjusted to 1,000 miles
•Reported adjusted (non-GAAP) net income of $40 million, or $0.18 per diluted share, significantly exceeding guidance despite an average fuel price that was $0.26 per gallon higher than assumed when guidance was issued
•Ended the third quarter 2026 with $1.18 billion of total liquidity, up slightly from the prior quarter
•Executed agreements in the third quarter to early return 13 A320neo leases in the second half of 2026 and cost efficiently replace that capacity with the direct lease of 10 A321neo aircraft scheduled to be delivered by the first quarter of 2027

“Frontier’s revenue execution in a higher than expected fuel environment resulted in an adjusted profit, meaningfully above the Company's guidance and Wall Street estimates,” said Jimmy Dempsey, President and Chief Executive Officer. “The Company delivered record revenue of $1.4 billion, up 53 percent versus the prior year, due to strong travel demand and the continued progression of the Company's revenue management initiatives, while maintaining cost discipline. These results reflect the successful progression of the Company's strategic initiatives set out earlier this year, and it gives us confidence in our ability to translate our momentum into sustainable profitability.”
Revenue and Cost Performance
Revenue was approximately $1.4 billion in the third quarter of 2026, a Company record, driven by strong travel demand and the continued progression of the Company's revenue management initiatives. RASM increased 30 percent over the corresponding 2025 quarter to 11.87 cents, significantly above the guidance range, on ASMs that were 11.4 billion, 18 percent higher compared to the corresponding 2025 quarter on an average stage length of 891 miles and a flown load factor of 79 percent.
Total adjusted (non-GAAP) operating expenses were approximately $1.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026, or 11.56 cents per ASM. This includes $425 million of fuel expense at an average cost of $3.96 per gallon. The average cost per gallon reflects an increase of 56 percent versus the corresponding 2025 quarter, or approximately $150 million of incremental expense on current quarter fuel consumption.
Total adjusted (non-GAAP) operating expenses excluding fuel in the third quarter of 2026 were $896 million, or 7.40 cents per ASM, stage-adjusted to 1,000 miles. Refer to “Reconciliations of Non-GAAP Financial Information” in the appendix of this release.
Earnings
Net loss (GAAP) for the third quarter of 2026 was $104 million. Adjusted (non-GAAP) net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $40 million, or $0.18 per diluted share based on approximately 233 million weighted-average shares outstanding in the quarter.

Frontier took delivery of one A320neo aircraft and three A321neo aircraft during the third quarter of 2026. The Company also returned all 13 A320neo aircraft associated with the early return agreement as previously disclosed, with five returns in the third quarter of 2026 and the remainder in October 2026.

About Frontier Airlines
Frontier Airlines, Inc., a subsidiary of Frontier Group Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: ULCC), is a value-focused airline operating one of the youngest and most fuel-efficient fleets in the United States. With a growing network and continued investment in product upgrades, including the introduction of First Class seating, onboard Wi-Fi and enhanced loyalty benefits, Frontier is giving customers more comfort, flexibility, and rewards while keeping fares low.
