VOC Energy Trust Announces Trust Quarterly Distribution

 

VOC ENERGY TRUST

The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A., Trustee

NEWS RELEASE

 

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

 

Houston, Texas, October 20, 2026 — VOC Energy Trust (NYSE Symbol — VOC) announced the Trust distribution of net profits for the quarterly payment period ended September 30, 2026.

 

Unitholders of record on October 30, 2026 will receive a distribution amounting to $3,230,000 or $0.19 per unit, payable November 13, 2026.
