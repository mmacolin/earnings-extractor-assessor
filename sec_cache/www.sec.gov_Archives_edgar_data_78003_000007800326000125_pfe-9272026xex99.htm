Pfizer Reports Strong Third-Quarter 2026 Results And Raises 2026 Guidance
■Focused Execution Drove 32% Operational Revenue Growth of Launched and Acquired Products(1)
■Advancing Robust Late-Stage Pipeline with Multiple Key Catalysts Ahead
■Raises 2026 Guidance(2) on Strong Year-to-Date Performance
■Appoints Cecile Guegan Chief Financial Officer, Executive Vice President
NEW YORK, Tuesday, November 3, 2026 — Pfizer Inc. (NYSE: PFE) reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026 and raised its full-year 2026 guidance(2) for both Revenues and Adjusted(3) diluted EPS.
EXECUTIVE COMMENTARY
Dr. Albert Bourla, Chairman and CEO of Pfizer:
"Pfizer had another strong quarter with commercial momentum and disciplined execution while advancing our strategy for creating long-term shareholder value. Our obesity program continued to advance and recent milestones in oncology, immunology and vaccines further demonstrate the potential of our pipeline. With a robust slate of expected catalysts ahead, I believe we're well positioned to deliver meaningful new medicines for patients and lasting value in the years ahead."

“Our third-quarter results reflect strong commercial performance globally and continued operational discipline. QUARTERLY FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS (Third-Quarter 2026 vs. Third-Quarter 2025)
Third-quarter 2026 revenues totaled $16.6 billion, a decrease of $71 million, or relatively flat on a percentage basis compared to the prior-year quarter, reflecting an operational decrease of $57 million and an unfavorable impact of foreign exchange of $15 million. The operational decrease was driven by a decline in revenues from COVID-19 products, the Prevnar family and several other products across categories, largely offset by an increase in revenues for Eliquis, Padcev, the Vyndaqel family, Nurtec, Lorbrena and several other products across categories. Excluding contributions from Comirnaty and Paxlovid, revenues for the third quarter grew 7% operationally. Additionally, third-quarter revenues of our Launched and Acquired Products(1) grew 32% operationally.
Third-quarter 2026 operational revenue reflected higher revenues primarily for:
■Eliquis globally, up $513 million, or 25%, operationally, driven primarily by higher net price in the U.S. primarily due to pricing dynamics, including lower rebates and channel mix favorability, as well as higher demand globally; partially offset by declines due to generic entry and price erosion in certain international markets;
■Padcev globally, up $255 million, or 55%, operationally, driven primarily by increased market share in first-line locally advanced or metastatic urothelial cancer (la/mUC), as well as launch uptake in the cisplatin-ineligible indication for muscle-invasive bladder cancer;
■Vyndaqel family (Vyndaqel, Vyndamax, Vynmac) globally, up $193 million, or 12%, operationally, driven primarily by continued market expansion in the U.S., as well as strong demand with continuing uptake in patient diagnosis across international markets and improved access in certain international markets;
■Nurtec ODT/Vydura globally, up $135 million, or 33%, operationally, driven primarily by strong demand in the U.S., as well as launch uptake in certain international markets; and
■Lorbrena globally, up $114 million, or 43%, operationally, driven primarily by increased patient share in the first-line ALK-positive metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (ALK+ mNSCLC) treatment setting in the U.S., China, and certain other international markets;

Elrexfio (elranatamab)Phase 3 Results
October 2026. Announced topline results from the investigational Phase 3 MagnetisMM-32 study evaluating Elrexfio as monotherapy in adults with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (RRMM) who received at least one prior line of treatment with anti-CD38 therapy and lenalidomide. At the pre-specified interim analysis for efficacy, the study met its primary endpoint of progression-free survival (PFS) as assessed by blinded independent central review (BICR). Elrexfio demonstrated a statistically significant and clinically meaningful reduction in the risk of disease progression or death versus investigator’s choice of elotuzumab, pomalidomide and dexamethasone (EloPd); pomalidomide, bortezomib and dexamethasone (PVd); or carfilzomib and dexamethasone (Kd). The safety profile of Elrexfio in MagnetisMM-32 was consistent with its known profile and no new safety signals were identified.

Tilrekimig
(PF-07275315)		Phase 2 Results
October 2026. Presented detailed results from an ongoing Phase 2 study investigating the potentially first-in-class trispecific antibody tilrekimig in adults with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis (AD). The study met its primary endpoint, demonstrating a statistically significant increase in the percentage of participants achieving EASI-75 (≥75% reduction in the Eczema Area and Severity Index) at Week 16 across all evaluated doses compared to placebo. Tilrekimig was well-tolerated, with no dose-dependent safety signals, with treatment-emergent adverse event rates comparable between treatment and placebo groups.
