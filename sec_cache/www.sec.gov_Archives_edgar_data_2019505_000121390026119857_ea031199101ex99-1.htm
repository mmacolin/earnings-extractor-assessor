Health In Tech Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Contracted Revenue of $32.7 Million for the First Nine Months of 2026
Pipeline Revenue of $156.4 Million as of October 30, 2026
Distribution Partners Grew 15.9% Year Over Year

 

Stuart, FL., November 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ — Health In Tech, Inc. (Nasdaq: HIT) (“Health In Tech” or the “Company”), an AI-enabled InsurTech platform company, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter and Nine-Month 2026 Highlights

 

●Distribution Partners, including brokers, third-party administrators (“TPAs”) and agencies, reached 984 as of September 30, 2026, an increase of 15.9% year over year.

 

●Q3 2026 Revenue was $6.6 million, compared with $8.5 million in Q3 2025. Nine-month 2026 revenue was $23.4 million, compared with $25.8 million in the prior year period.

 

●Contracted Revenue1 totaled $32.7 million for the first nine months of 2026, of which $24.4 million was recognized as GAAP revenue, before the enrollment adjustment and refund liability. The remaining $6.1 million and $2.2 million are expected to be recognized as GAAP revenue in the fourth quarter of 2026 and in 2027, respectively.

 

●Pipeline Revenue2 as of October 30, 2026 was $156.4 million, of which $2.6 million was contracted subsequent to quarter end. The remaining $153.8 million represents policies in quoting or binding status, with an expected conversion rate of 3% to 7%.

 

●Net Loss for Q3 2026 was $7.1 million, or $(0.11) per diluted share, and included a provision for credit losses of $5.3 million, compared to net income of $0.5 million, or $0.01 per diluted share, in Q3 2025. Net loss for the first nine months of 2026 was $11.2 million, or $(0.18) per diluted share, and included a provision for credit losses of $6.1 million, compared to net income of $1.6 million, or $0.03 per diluted share, for the first nine months of 2025.

 

●Adjusted EBITDA3 was $(3.9) million for Q3 2026 and $(6.6) million for the first nine months of 2026, reflecting continued investment in distribution, technology, and product development.

 

●Platform Placed Plan Value (“PPPV”)4 was $91.8 million as of September 30, 2026.

 

●Cash balance was $10.1 million as of September 30, 2026.

Corporate and Strategic Highlights

 

●HitRix Launch. Launched HitRix in September 2026, an AI-powered, broker-first marketplace designed to modernize the large-group self-funded stop-loss insurance placement process. By automating data ingestion, streamlining submissions across multiple carriers and managing general underwriters (“MGUs”), and enabling more efficient proposal comparisons, HitRix aims to reduce manual workflows, enhance broker productivity, improve quoting efficiency, and drive higher policy placement conversion rates.

●Three-Year Rate Stabilization Program. With the initial launch targeted by year-end 2026, the Company is prioritizing disciplined execution and aims to establish a foundation for converting its pipeline into future commercial growth.

●Updated 2026 Revenue Outlook. The Company revised its full-year 2026 revenue outlook to approximately $30 million, modestly below 2025 revenue. The revision primarily reflects the delayed commencement of certain new policies resulting from the transition to a new carrier, which temporarily extended underwriting and onboarding timelines. These delays shifted the anticipated revenue contribution from certain new business opportunities into 2027, rather than reflecting a loss of the underlying opportunities. With the carrier transition substantially completed, the Company is focused on accelerating policy onboarding, improving conversion rates, and translating its existing pipeline into revenue growth in 2027.

 

End Notes

 

1.Contracted Revenue is a period-based operating metric and is not a point-in-time backlog measure. Contracted Revenue includes contractual revenue associated with policies that are in force at any time during the applicable reporting period. For each such policy, Contracted Revenue includes: contractual revenue attributable to the portion of the policy term falling within the reporting period, including the amount recognized as GAAP revenue during the reporting period; and remaining contractual revenue expected to be recognized in future periods over the remaining contractual term of that policy. Revenue attributable to periods before the beginning of the applicable reporting period is excluded. A policy that expired or lapsed before the beginning of the reporting period is also excluded. If a policy expires or lapses during the reporting period, Contracted Revenue includes revenue attributable to that policy only through its expiration or lapse date, and no revenue is included for periods thereafter. Standard self-funded plan policies generally have a contractual term of 12 months, while our Three-Year Rate Stabilization Program is designed with a 36-month contractual term. Revenue is recognized under U.S. GAAP on a straight-line basis over the policy term, beginning on the policy’s effective date. Accordingly, Contracted Revenue includes contractual revenue associated with policies that are in force at any time during the applicable reporting period. If a policy expires or lapses during the reporting period, Contracted Revenue includes revenue attributable to that policy only through its expiration or lapse date, and no revenue is included for periods thereafter. Contracted revenue is presented prior to the impact of monthly employers’ enrollment adjustments during the contractual period, including changes in enrolled employees, plan mix, and other factors that may affect premium and fees. Accordingly, actual revenue recognized in future periods may differ from Contracted Revenue. Contracted Revenue is an operating metric and is not a substitute for GAAP revenue.

4.Platform Placed Plan Value (“PPPV”) represents the aggregate contractual value of self-funded health plans with stop-loss insurance (self-funded stop-loss plans) placed through the Company’s platform during the fiscal year through the applicable fiscal quarter end, measured over each plan’s full contractual term of typically 12 or 36 months from the plan’s effective date. PPPV reflects the total economic value flowing through the platform, including premium, claim funding, and administrative fees, and is a measure of platform transaction volume rather than an indication of the Company’s own revenue or take rate.

About Health In Tech

 

Health In Tech, Inc. (Nasdaq: HIT) is an AI-enabled InsurTech platform company, which offers a marketplace that improves processes in the health insurance industry through vertical integration, process simplification, and automation. By removing friction and complexities, we streamline the underwriting, sales and service process for insurance companies, licensed brokers, Managing General Underwriter (“MGUs”) and third-party administrators (“TPAs”). Health In Tech’s platform serves as a marketplace for brokers, TPAs, MGUs and carriers to access self-funded health insurance for employers, providing functions including customized self-funded health plans, bindable stop-loss quotes, AI-enabled underwriting, claims administration and reporting integration.
