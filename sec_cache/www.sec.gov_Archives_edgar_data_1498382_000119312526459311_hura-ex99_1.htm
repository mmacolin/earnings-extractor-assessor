TuHURA Biosciences Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides a Corporate Update

 

TAMPA, Fla., Nov. 13, 2026 -- TuHURA Biosciences, Inc. (NASDAQ:HURA) (“TuHURA” or the “Company”), a Phase 3 immuno-oncology company developing novel therapeutics to overcome resistance to cancer immunotherapy, today reported financial results for the Company's third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

"We continue to make significant progress across each of our programs. In October, we shared our first look at data from our open-label front line study of IFx-2.0 with Keytruda® in patients with deep-seated metastatic Merkel cell carcinoma, which showed durable, major objective anti-tumor responses in the first three patients with all responses ongoing for more than six months," said Dr. James Bianco, President and Chief Executive Officer of TuHURA Biosciences. "Working collaboratively with the FDA, we received clearance to begin our Phase 1b study of TBS-2025, our VISTA-inhibiting antibody, in relapsed/refractory mutNPM1 acute myeloid leukemia (AML), a molecularly defined subset of AML where the presence of VISTA on leukemic cells is associated with poor response to therapy and survival.”

Dr. Bianco continued, “We will have a strong presence again this year at the upcoming American Society of Hematology (ASH) Annual Meeting in December with 3 poster presentations highlighting our Delta Opioid Receptor (DOR) technology. The ASH scientific review committee recognized the importance of our discoveries of the central role the DOR plays in controlling proliferation, recruitment and immunosuppressive capabilities of MDSCs, the body’s main cell type for suppressing immune responses leading to acquired resistance to cancer immunotherapies.”

“With meaningful progress having been made in each of our programs, we believe we are well positioned to deliver on several transformational potential milestones. Having continuing access to a $50mm credit facility from our largest shareholder has allowed us to fund operations in a non-dilutive manner benefiting shareholders as we continue driving towards these value-creating events,” Dr. Bianco concluded.

Third Quarter and Recent Corporate Highlights:

 

•
Announced three poster presentations at the 68th American Society of Hematology (ASH) Annual Meeting and Exposition taking place December 12-15, 2026, in New Orleans. The posters highlight research on the role of the Delta Opioid Receptor (DOR) in controlling the immunosuppressive capabilities of

 

myeloid-derived suppressor cells (MDSCs), and the data underscore the potential therapeutic breadth of DOR technology across MDSCs, T regs and M2 macrophages.

Provided a first look at data from its open-label study of IFx-2.0 as an adjunctive therapy to Keytruda® in first line treatment of patients with deep seated metastatic Merkel Cell Carcinoma. Preliminary results demonstrated major objective partial responses in each of the first 3 patients, constituting major objective tumor responses, which are ongoing for more than six months.

 

•
Received a new U.S. Patent broadening coverage for its VISTA Inhibitor, TBS-2025. The patent extends coverage to include bispecific/multi-specific antibodies across all classes of human antibodies as well as coverage for methods of treatment that extend beyond cancer, expanding into new treatment areas like autoimmune disease and infection. The patent term extends to 2042, prior to any potential patent term adjustment and/or regulatory patent term extension.

 

•
Received key U.S. patent for its groundbreaking Delta Opioid Receptor (DOR) Antibody Drug Conjugate (ADC) technology. The patent discloses uses beyond ADCs, including autologous or allogeneic adoptive cellular immunotherapies like CAR-T, Tregs or TIL cell therapies. The term of the patent runs through 2043 before any patent term extension.

 

•
Well funded to achieve clinical goals. The Parkview loan facility contributed $5.6 million in non-dilutive financing during the quarter ended September 30, 2026. This facility allows the Company to operationally proceed on all fronts while maintaining shareholder value. This facility also allows the Company to focus exclusively on operational goals.

 

•
Received FDA "Study May Proceed" notice and IND clearance for the evaluation of its VISTA-Inhibiting Antibody in molecularly defined subsets of AML and other blood-related cancers. The IND allows the Company to examine TBS-2025 in a Phase 1b study evaluating the safety and potential efficacy of monotherapy at different doses in patients with relapsed/refractory (r/r) mutNPM1 AML who have failed to respond to or relapsed after Menin inhibitor therapy. If TBS-2025 results in encouraging response rates during the dose-optimization stage of the study, the

 

Company will explore with the FDA the potential expansion of the trial in consideration for the opportunity for accelerated approval.

About TuHURA Biosciences, Inc.
TuHURA Biosciences, Inc. (NASDAQ: HURA) is a Phase 3 immuno-oncology company developing novel technologies to overcome primary and acquired resistance to cancer immunotherapy, two of the most common reasons cancer immunotherapies fail to work or stop working in the majority of patients with cancer.

 

TuHURA's lead innate immune agonist, IFx-2.0, is designed to overcome primary resistance to checkpoint inhibitors. TuHURA has initiated a single randomized placebo-controlled Phase 3 registration trial of IFx-2.0 administered as an adjunctive therapy to Keytruda® (pembrolizumab) compared to Keytruda® plus placebo in first-line treatment for advanced or metastatic Merkel Cell Carcinoma.

 

In addition to its innate immune agonist product candidates, TuHURA is developing TBS-2025, its VISTA-inhibiting mAb in a Phase 1b clinical trial in patients with mutNPM1 r/r AML, a molecularly defined subgroup that represents approximately 35% of patients with AML. TuHURA is also leveraging its Delta Opioid Receptor technology to develop first-in-class, bi-specific, bi-functional antibody drug conjugates (ADCs) targeting Myeloid-Derived Suppressor Cells to inhibit their immune-suppressing effects on the tumor microenvironment to prevent T cell exhaustion and acquired resistance to checkpoint inhibitors and cellular therapies.
