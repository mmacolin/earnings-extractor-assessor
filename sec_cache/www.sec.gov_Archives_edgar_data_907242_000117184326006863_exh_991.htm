John Farahi, Co-Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Monarch, commented: “In the third quarter of 2026, Monarch delivered record quarterly net revenue and adjusted EBITDA. Net revenue increased 2.4% year-over-year to $146.3 million, reflecting growth in casino, food and beverage, and hotel revenues. Quarterly Adjusted EBITDA grew 2.1% compared to the same period last year. The third-quarter 2026 adjusted EBITDA margin remained near record levels at 38.3%. The third quarter net revenue and adjusted EBITDA growth highlights our ability to drive sustained growth from our two properties.

Summary of 2026 Third Quarter Operating Results
In the third quarter of 2026, the Company generated net revenue of $146.3 million compared to $142.8 million in the corresponding prior-year period. Casino revenue increased 3.4%, food and beverage (“F&B”) revenue increased 1.3% and hotel revenue increased 0.9%, compared to the same prior-year period.

Selling, general and administrative (“SG&A”) expense for the third quarter of 2026 was $29.0 million compared to $27.5 million in the corresponding prior-year period. As a percentage of net revenue, SG&A expense increased to 19.8% from 19.3% in the corresponding prior-year period. The increase in SG&A expenses is primarily the result of an increase in legal and employee related expenses. Casino operating expense as a percentage of casino revenue was unchanged at 35.8% during the third quarter of 2026 compared to the corresponding prior-year period. During the third quarter of 2026, F&B operating expense as a percentage of F&B revenue decreased slightly to 69.6% from 69.9% in the corresponding prior-year period due to an increase in revenue per cover. Hotel operating expense as a percentage of hotel revenue decreased slightly to 31.3% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 31.4% in the corresponding prior-year period, primarily due to an increase in Average Daily Rate and a decrease in operating expenses per occupied room.

Net income for the third quarter of 2026 increased 1.4% and diluted EPS increased 4.1% compared to the corresponding prior-year period. The Company generated consolidated Adjusted EBITDA of $56.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, which represents a $1.2 million, or 2.1% increase, compared to the same prior-year period.

Credit Facility and Liquidity
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had cash and cash equivalents of $137.4 million and no borrowings against its credit facility.

Capital expenditures of $9.7 million in the third quarter of 2026 were funded from operating cash flow and included costs related to ongoing maintenance capital projects, costs related to the purchase of new gaming equipment at both properties and purchase of land with a building adjacent to Atlantis.

On September 15, 2026, the Company paid a cash dividend of $0.30 per share to its stockholders of record as of September 1, 2026 for a total of $5.3 million. The cash dividend was funded from operating cash flow.

In the third quarter of 2026, the Company purchased in the open market, under its share repurchase plan, 300,859 shares of its common stock, for $37 million. The purchases were funded from operating cash flow. As of September 30, 2026, the Company has 670,644 shares remaining of the 3,000,000 shares originally authorized for repurchase by the Board.

Monarch believes its strong balance sheet and free cash flow favorably position the Company to continue investing in its properties, share repurchases and paying cash dividends. The Company continues to diligently evaluate potential M&A transactions, which, it believes, if completed, could drive additional long-term value for stockholders.

Quarterly Dividend Declaration
The Company today announced a cash dividend of $0.30 per share of its outstanding common stock. The dividend is payable on December 15, 2026 to stockholders of record as of December 1, 2026. This cash dividend is part of the previously announced annual cash dividend of $1.20 per share payable in quarterly payments and subject to quarterly review and evaluation by the Company’s Board of Directors.

About Monarch Casino & Resort, Inc.
Monarch Casino & Resort, Inc., through its subsidiaries, owns and operates the Monarch Casino Resort Spa ("Monarch Black Hawk") in Black Hawk, Colorado, approximately 40 miles west of Denver and the Atlantis Casino Resort Spa ("Atlantis"), a hotel/casino facility in Reno, Nevada. For additional information on Monarch, visit the Company's website at www.monarchcasino.com.

Atlantis features 817 guest rooms and suites, and approximately 61,000 square feet of casino space. The casino features approximately 1,200 slot and video poker machines; approximately 30 table games, including blackjack, craps, roulette, and others; a race and sports book; a 24-hour live keno lounge; and a poker room. It also includes eight food outlets; two gourmet coffee and pastry bars; a retail store; a 30,000 square foot health spa and salon with an enclosed year-round pool; an 8,000 square-foot family entertainment center; and approximately 52,000 square feet of banquet, convention and meeting room space.

Monarch Black Hawk features 516 guest rooms and suites, and approximately 60,000 square feet of casino space. The resort offers approximately 1,100 slot machines; 37 table games; a poker room; keno; and a sports book. It also includes 10 bars and lounges, as well as four dining options: a twenty-four-hour full-service restaurant, a buffet-style restaurant, the Monarch Chophouse (a fine-dining steakhouse), and Bistro Mariposa (elevated Southwest cuisine), banquet and meeting room space, a retail store, a concierge lounge and an upscale spa and enclosed year-round pool located on the top floor of the tower. The resort is connected to a nine-story parking structure with approximately 1,350 parking spaces, and additional valet parking, with total property capacity of approximately 1,500 spaces.
